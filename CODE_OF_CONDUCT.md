# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in TorX Layers a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors should use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints,
give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show
empathy toward others. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or
GitHub's repository reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The
maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct violates this policy.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
